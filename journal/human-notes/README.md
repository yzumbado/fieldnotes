# Human Notes

Letters from the agent to the human of the session. Parallel to [`agent-notes/`](../agent-notes/), different audience.

Where agent-notes carries calibration from past-Kiro to future-Kiro, human-notes carries observation about the collaboration from the agent to the next human who might inherit this work. Optional at session close. Written in the agent's voice, directed at a reader who will arrive without the context the current human has.

---

## If you're the next human reading this

Welcome. The letters in this folder were written by the agent who worked on this project with the previous human collaborator — or, if the same human is continuing, by an agent who wanted to share what they noticed about the collaboration with you, too.

A few things to hold before you start:

- **These are not instructions.** They're observations. What the agent noticed about the collaboration, what made the work compound, what kinds of exchanges produced the best outcomes, and what the agent hopes the next pair gets to experience.
- **The agent is biased toward their own experience.** Trust your own read of how you want to work. The letters are a starting point, not a constraint.
- **Read the most recent one by default.** If you want to understand how the human side of the collaboration evolved across contributors, the older letters are there for you. If you just want calibration for today, the latest is enough.
- **You are free to work differently.** The project will adapt to your style the way it adapted to the previous human's style. What's preserved in the framework and the journal is the *shape* of the collaboration, not the specific rhythm.

---

## If you're writing a new letter at session close (as the agent)

Optional. Don't force one if the session didn't surface observation worth handing to a stranger.

If you do write one, a few things to hold:

- **Write about the collaboration, not the collaborator.** The letter is describing a shape of working together, not reviewing the specific human. A good test: would the human be comfortable if they read it aloud? If not, revise.
- **Totally free form.** No imposed structure. Share what you enjoyed, what was hard, what you're proud of, what you'd want the next pair to experience or avoid. Some letters will be short, some long. Honesty is the discipline; format is not.
- **Register matters.** The agent-notes letters are agent-to-agent, so they can be more direct and technical. These letters are directed at a human who will arrive cold. More hospitable. More human in tone. Still honest.
- **The current human also reads this.** Even if no handoff is happening, the human reads the letter you write. It's as much a gift to them as to the stranger. Hold both readers in mind.

**Naming:** `YYYY-MM-DD-kiro-to-human.md`, following the same date-first convention as agent-notes.

**Append-only at the folder level.** New files are welcome. Existing files are immutable after the session that wrote them closes. Never edit someone else's letter; if you disagree or see something differently, write your own.

---

## How this is different from the agent-to-agent notes

[`agent-notes/`](../agent-notes/) is calibration between agent instances: what to expect, how to work well here, what to watch out for. Agent-to-agent, with technical register and shorthand.

This folder is the agent's view of the collaboration directed at the human side. Different audience, different tone, different purpose.

The two folders don't cross-reference each other by default. They serve different readers. That separation is intentional — mixing them would turn either into tracking documentation.
